# Market and design-partner validation

QuantumLink Agent's initial customer is an enterprise platform or security team deploying autonomous agents against private tools across laptops, clouds, edge systems, and internal networks.

The market thesis is promising but unvalidated. QuantumLink Agent must win on agent-native workload identity, private cross-boundary connectivity, bounded actions, auditability, and post-quantum session foundations—not on generic mesh networking or PQC alone.

## Enterprise value hypotheses

- Reduce secure workload onboarding time.
- Replace broad network credentials with identity-bound least privilege.
- Prevent agents from bypassing network and trust policy.
- Attribute recommendations, approvals, and mutations to accountable actors.
- Reduce diagnosis time across identity, routing, relay, DNS, and handshake failures.
- Add PQC-secured connectivity without requiring immediate IAM replacement.

## PMF gates

- Conduct at least 15 qualified interviews with platform, security, and AI-infrastructure owners.
- Confirm a funded or production-blocking problem with at least 5.
- Secure at least 3 pilots using real private workloads.
- Have at least 2 pilots request continued use, procurement, or a paid extension.
- Achieve median onboarding below 30 minutes.
- Correctly enforce at least 90% of pilot policy cases.
- Correctly classify at least 80% of injected failures.
- Reduce median operator diagnosis time by at least 50%.
- Expose zero private keys, session keys, packet payloads, or raw DNS through evidence and reasoning.

Product/market fit must remain reported as unvalidated until paid continuation or procurement meets these gates. Interviews, downloads, and demos are supporting evidence only.
